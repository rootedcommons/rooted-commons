# Rooted Commons v2.4.0

## Product catalogue

- Reworks product cards into the agreed hierarchy: photograph, sentence-case product name, preparation/location line, grown-in line, subdued certification marks, then weight and purchasing controls.
- Removes producer names and country flags from product cards.
- Keeps weight at the lower left; price sits above the quantity selector at the lower right.
- Makes the image and title open an accessible expanded product dialog when additional information exists.
- Adds support for Products fields: Expanded description, Ingredients, Allergens, Storage, Suggested uses, Why we stock it and Source URL.
- Removes Gluten Free from the certification-logo map.

## Member summary

- Restyles the small member summary above the shop toolbar as a deliberate compact account strip.
- Separates current credit and collection point into clearly labelled values.
- Adds an inline Change collection point control using the existing secure member PATCH endpoint.
