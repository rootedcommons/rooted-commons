# Rooted Commons v2.9.11 setup

## Membership card

The 60/40 badge/credit structure remains.

The right-hand 40% column now anchors its content at the bottom:
- Member credit
- Browse this week's market

Membership weeks have moved back into the Membership card immediately before the perks section:
- `x weeks`
- `supporting local producers`

This restores the visual link between membership duration and perks.

## Weekly commitment

`dashboard.regular_payment_note` is no longer used.

The existing `dashboard.reference_help` copy is reused directly beneath the member's RC-x bank reference.

## Collection point

`dashboard.collection_view` is no longer used. This does not affect the Change collection point button.

The `(see our map)` link beside the collection-point selector remains.

## Your Involvement

The former Your Impact card is now **Your Involvement** and uses equal 50/50 columns.

Counters:
- £ redirected locally
- volunteer days / volunteering in your local food network
- workshops attended
- events attended

Add to Members:
- `Events attended` — Number

Existing fields:
- `Volunteer days` — Number
- `Workshops attended` — Number

Blank values render as 0.

At the bottom is the collective-impact bridge:
- `You’re part of something bigger.`
- `See what our network is growing, sharing and giving back →`

The destination is editable through `dashboard.bigger_url` and defaults to `/our-partners/`.
