# Rooted Commons v2.9.12 legal-page setup

## Dedicated coded pages

The following routes now exist independently of the Baserow Pages table:

- `/privacy/`
- `/membership-terms/`
- `/collection-points/`
- `/contact/`
- `/terms-of-sale/`

They are explicitly excluded from the generic `[slug].astro` Pages-table route.

After v2.9.12 is deployed, rows with these slugs can be removed from the Baserow Pages table without deleting the actual routes.

Note: if your Header is falling back to automatically building navigation from the Pages table rather than using Site Settings → Navigation links, deleting a Pages row can also remove that item from the automatically generated header navigation. Direct links elsewhere on the site continue to work.

## Terms of Sale

`/terms-of-sale/` is a dedicated coded page covering:
- Rooted Commons member purchases;
- direct local sales, including small business sales;
- member-credit vs invoice/agreed payment methods;
- availability and substitutions;
- order changes;
- a voluntary 14-day cancellation period for eligible non-perishable distance purchases for all customers;
- the perishable-goods exception;
- collection/delivery;
- problems with goods and statutory rights;
- a model cancellation form.

The footer now links to Terms of Sale.

## Collection points

Collection points is now a dedicated route instead of relying on a Pages-table row. Its title and intro are editable through Interface Content:

- `collection_points.title`
- `collection_points.intro`
