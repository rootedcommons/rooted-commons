# Rooted Commons v2.9.9 setup

## Dashboard structure

Desktop/tablet:
1. Your Membership
2. Your Weekly Commitment
3. Your Collection Point
4. Your Impact

The first and second rows are two-column grids and stack on narrow screens.

## Membership card

The card now contains:

- `Hi {member}`
- `Your membership`
- a 60% / 40% responsive row:
  - left: Founder / Member badge image
  - right: Member credit, left-aligned and kept on one line
- negative-credit top-up chooser when needed
- Browse this week's market
- existing member perks

The text `Founder 10` / `Founder 25` / `Founder 50` is not repeated beneath the badge.

The previous-order line has been removed entirely.

## Your Impact

The former `x weeks` and `£x redirected locally` counters now live in the bottom-right `Your impact` card.

The card uses the same 60% / 40% column ratio and currently displays:

- membership weeks / supporting local producers
- £ redirected locally
- volunteer days / volunteering in your local food network
- workshops attended

Add these Members fields:

- `Volunteer days` — Number
- `Workshops attended` — Number

If they are absent or blank the dashboard safely displays 0.

## Collection point image

The collection-point image now uses its natural aspect ratio. Width responds to the card width and height follows the image proportionally; it is not cropped into a fixed-height box.
