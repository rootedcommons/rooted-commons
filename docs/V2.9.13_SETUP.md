# Rooted Commons v2.9.13 legal-content update

No Baserow schema changes are required.

## Privacy notice

`/privacy/` has been revised to:
- identify Roots to Fruits CIC clearly as data controller;
- remove the named provider list, including obsolete MailerLite references;
- describe recipients by appropriate categories instead;
- clarify required membership data versus optional communications;
- distinguish essential membership/service messages from consent-based optional news;
- improve retention wording so full membership data is not implied to be retained for the accounting-record period;
- state accurately that local storage is used for basket, collection preferences and secure access;
- clarify direct-marketing and consent rights.

Provider-level processing information should be maintained internally rather than requiring the public notice to name every software supplier.

## Membership terms

`/membership-terms/` has been reduced from 11 sections to 7.

The revised terms:
- keep the distinction between Rooted Commons membership and statutory/company membership of Roots to Fruits CIC;
- keep the £10 weekly / £43.33 monthly minimum commitment prominent;
- separate the ongoing membership relationship from individual sales by linking to `/terms-of-sale/`;
- simplify ordering and collection provisions;
- replace the previous non-withdrawable Member Credit language with a fairer exit arrangement;
- allow a partner voucher only where the leaving member agrees to it;
- simplify liability language;
- distinguish reasonable operational changes from significant changes to the membership bargain;
- allow a member to leave if they do not wish to continue after a significant change.

## Signup-form wording

This release does not alter the signup form consent wording. A future form/content update should ensure the final acceptance statement makes the regular commitment sufficiently prominent, for example:

`I agree to the Membership Terms, including my chosen regular commitment to Rooted Commons.`

Do not change this automatically if the current form copy is intentionally managed through Interface Content.
