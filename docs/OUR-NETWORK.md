# Our Network page

The Our Network page connects a member's personal dashboard story to the collective story of Rooted Commons.

## Recommended page order

1. Page hero / introductory text: explain that the network is made from growers, makers, community organisations, collection points and shared infrastructure.
2. `Stats` section: link four defensible network-wide rows from **Metrics**.
3. `Network` section: render active **Network Partners** as compact clickable cards with expanded profiles.
4. `Call to action`: route people into buying, volunteering, workshops, growing, hosting a collection point or supplying the network.

## Metrics

Use the single **Metrics** table for public network-level measures. A Stats section explicitly links the exact Metric rows it should display through **Sections → Metrics**; the rows render in `Display order`.

Recommended initial Network metrics are: fresh produce donated; qualifying spend kept in the local economy; volunteer hours; and people taking part. Keep the underlying counting method in `Calc method` and evidence provenance in `Evidence / source` so the figures can later be mapped defensibly to external social-value frameworks without double counting.

`Placement` is metadata (`Home`, `Network`, `Partner`, `Dashboard`) rather than the sole selection mechanism. Partner profile measures require both `Placement = Partner` and the relevant `Network Partner` link.

TOM metadata is recorded separately from the Rooted Commons metric itself: `TOM Theme` (`Work`, `Economy`, `Community`, `Planet`), `TOM Outcome`, and `TOM Measure`. Leave Outcome/Measure blank unless the mapping is defensible against the current external taxonomy.

## Partner cards

The `Network` section reads **Network Partners**, not generic Cards. The compact card can contain a role, summary and up to three images. Clicking **Explore** opens the expanded profile.

Expanded profiles can contain: Long description; What they bring; How we work together; Address; Website; Volunteer URL; Social URL; Get involved label/URL; images; and partner-specific Metrics.

Partner-level statistics are optional. Do not invent figures merely to fill the layout.
